The latest contribution to [The Supernatural Writings of Stuart Palmer](/pages/stuart-palmer/) is an ostensibly non-fiction article, from the June 1929 issue of *Ghost Stories*.

In [**How a Spirit Corrected the Standard Dictionary**](/stuart-palmer/how-a-spirit-corrected-the-standard-dictionary/), Palmer relates how the ghost of [Henry Ward Beecher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Ward_Beecher) sends a message to his friend [Isaac Funk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_K._Funk), during a seance. Funk was co-founder of the publishing house [Funk & Wagnalls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funk_%26_Wagnalls), who produced a number of authoritative reference works, including the Funk & Wagnalls Dictionary.

According to the story, Funk and his colleagues inadvertently misplaced a valuable ancient coin, and made an error in verifying its authenticity, to boot. So Beecher had to send a message from the Beyond to fix the situation.

This is supposedly the incident that converted Isaac Funk to a believer in Spiritualism. He wrote about the events in his book [*The Widow's Mite and Other Psychic Phenomena*](https://archive.org/details/widowsmiteother00funk/page/156/mode/2up) (1904). I imagine that Palmer's account is based largely on Dr. Funk's book.

Personally, I prefer Palmer's short stories to his "true ghost experience" articles. But those interested in Forteana and similar subjects might find this an interesting and readable account of an allegedly real-life psychic phenomenon. And if you are interested in reading more, there's always Funk's own account, linked above--complete with attestations, possible explanations, and extended discussion.
